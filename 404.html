Who I Am

I'm a Computer Science and Business Administration student at Northeastern University who likes building things that work in the real world, from machine learning models to a Lego-armored robot car. I chose to combine the two because I'm interested in how technology turns data into decisions, and I've seen that firsthand building Power BI dashboards and traffic data analyses as an IT intern at the Arkansas Department of Transportation.

Before that, I built a parking-space image classifier in Python and TensorFlow at the University of Arkansas at Little Rock, and in my own time I engineered a joystick-controlled turret for a Raspberry Pi robot with no tutorial to follow. Next, I want to keep growing at the intersection of software, data, and business, and I'm always up for a hard problem I haven't solved before.
