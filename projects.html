PiCar-X Lego Turret

A Raspberry Pi PiCar-X robot car rebuilt with Lego armor, a joystick-controlled servo turret, and a button-triggered Lego missile launcher, all still drivable from the mobile app. I wired the joystick straight into the Robot HAT's built-in ADC and merged app control and turret control into a single Python loop.

Machine Learning · Internship

Parking Space & Image Classification

Python Developer, University of Arkansas at Little Rock · Summer 2025

A scikit-learn SVM, tuned with GridSearchCV, that labels parking-space images as empty or occupied for real-time camera use. I also built and compared Keras/TensorFlow CNNs on 25,000 Dogs vs. Cats images; the deeper model, with data augmentation, reached 95% test accuracy.
